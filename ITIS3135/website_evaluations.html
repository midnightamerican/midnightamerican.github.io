Website Evaluations

Apple.com

  • Contrast

    This website has a sleek, bright, and modern look with light colors that contrast with each other. It heavily uses white space with bold lack text to make everything readable. Apple.com uses shapes, some with rounded edges, whicch makes it stand apart from others.

  • Repition

    Uses the same fonts, button styles, and layouts appear across all product pages. Product sections follow a familiar pattern.

  • Alignment

    Everything on the website feels balanced. Text is aligned and centered and pages doesn't feel cluttered.

  • Proximity

    Product names and images are grouped closely together, so that viewers know what information goes with each other.

Lingscars.com

  • Contrast

    Contrast is used but, its chaotic. Lingscars uses bright and flashy colors that clash with each other. As wella s using flashy gifs and animations. Instead of helping users focus, the visual noise often makes text hard to read and overwhelms the eye.

  • Repition

    Fonts, colors, and icons vary across the page. Theres no consistancy across the page, every element feels likes its on its own instead of being part of a template or pattern. The styles vary wildly across the page, sometimes even within the same section.

  • Alignment

    Content and visuals appear scattered with little grid or spacing logic. Boxes, images, text, and menu items seem placed in an sporatic manner, not on a clear layout grid. This contributes to a sense of visual clutter and confusion.

  • Proximity

    Related content isn’t grouped: car offers, entertainment bits, contact info, animations, and extras all sit side by side. This makes scanning hard, because your eyes have to jump between unrelated items with no spacing logic. Poor proximity further reduces clarity and increases cognitive load.
